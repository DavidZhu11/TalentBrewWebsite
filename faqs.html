FAQ

COMMON  QUESTIONS

Who do We recuit for?

Talent Brew is exclusively focused on providing talent solutions for the beverage industry. We work with clients ranging from VC backed start-ups to publicly listed corporations. We serve verticals including Beer, Wine, Spirits, RTD, Cannabis, Coffee and Non-Alc, as well as Beverage Distributors and Retailers.

Do We conduct Confidential Searches?

Yes. All searches are conducted with the highest level of confidentiality for our clients and candidates. Additionally, we are positioned to assist clients in performing searches to replace a current employee who is no longer a fit, ensuring discrection at every step.

Which departments do we serve?

Though we are anchored in sales recruitment, our candidate pool spans Finance, Supply Chain, Human Resources, Operations, Transportation, Warehouse, Hospitality, R&D, and Executive Leadership.

what type of recruitment packages does talent brew offer?

We offer Retained Search and Recruitment Process Outsourcing (RPO). Whether you need to make a one-off key hire, or need to hire multiple positions and maintain ongoing recruiting support, we will work with you to find an option that is tailored for your company's unique needs.

Does it cost anything to be a candidate represented by talent brew?

No. We are contracted to find the best possible talent for our client partners - Talent Brew will never charge a canidate for being represented by our firm.
